DLT in Capital Markets: Moving from Proof of Concept to Production

3 min read · August 2026 · RegalWeise Consulting

After years of pilots, distributed ledger technology is delivering in production in specific corners of capital markets, notably repo, collateral mobility and tokenized funds. The lesson is that it works best where multiple parties need a shared, synchronised record.

Where DLT Is Proving Its Value

The strongest cases remove reconciliation and settlement friction between institutions rather than replacing internal systems.

  • Intraday and term repo with atomic delivery-versus-payment
  • Collateral transfer and mobility across venues
  • Issuance and servicing of digital bonds and fund units

What Still Holds Projects Back

Technology is rarely the blocker. Adoption depends on network effects and integration with existing market infrastructure.

  • Legal certainty on finality and ownership of tokenized instruments
  • A settlement asset — tokenized cash or central bank money — on the same ledger
  • Integration with front-to-back systems, custody and reporting

Our Advice

Start with a multi-party problem you already pay to reconcile, secure a consortium of counterparties, and design the interface to legacy platforms first.
