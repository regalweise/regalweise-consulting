Digital Asset Custody: What Institutions Need Before They Hold Tokens

3 min read · May 2026 · RegalWeise Consulting

Custody is the gate through which institutional capital enters digital assets. Holding a private key safely is a different problem from holding a security in a depository, and the controls need to reflect that.

Core Custody Capabilities

An institutional-grade solution combines technology, operations and legal structure.

  • Key management using hardware security modules or multi-party computation
  • Segregation of client assets and clear legal ownership
  • Governance workflows: approvals, whitelisting, transaction limits
  • Independent assurance, such as SOC reports and insurance arrangements

Build, Buy or Partner

Few banks will build the full stack. Most will select a specialist provider or a hybrid model.

  • Assess provider resilience, jurisdiction and regulatory status
  • Test disaster recovery and key-recovery procedures, not just policy documents
  • Integrate positions into existing risk, accounting and reporting systems

Our View

Custody decisions are hard to reverse. Run due diligence the way you would for a new securities depository or core banking vendor.
