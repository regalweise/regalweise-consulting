CBDCs and India's e₹: Where Retail and Wholesale Digital Currency Stand

3 min read · June 2026 · RegalWeise Consulting

Central bank digital currencies are central bank money in digital form. India's Reserve Bank launched pilots for wholesale and retail versions of the digital rupee (e₹) in late 2022, and the programme has continued to evolve.

Two Models, Two Purposes

Wholesale CBDCs target interbank and institutional settlement. Retail CBDCs target the public as a digital form of cash.

  • Wholesale: settlement of government securities and interbank transactions
  • Retail: person-to-person and merchant payments through bank-provided wallets
  • Programmability and offline capability remain active areas of exploration

What Banks Should Be Doing

Banks are the distribution layer for e₹, which makes readiness a practical matter.

  • Integrate wallets and ledgers with core and payments systems
  • Plan liquidity and reconciliation between deposits and CBDC
  • Decide where CBDC fits beside UPI and existing instant payment rails

The Takeaway

CBDC is less a competitor to existing rails than a new settlement asset. Banks that build the plumbing early will shape how it is used.
