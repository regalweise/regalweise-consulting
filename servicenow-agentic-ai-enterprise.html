ServiceNow and Agentic AI: From Workflow Automation to Autonomous Operations

3 min read · September 2026 · RegalWeise Consulting

ServiceNow built its franchise on workflows. Its agentic AI push extends that foundation: AI agents that reason, decide and act across IT, customer and risk processes, using the same platform data and controls.

What Changes with Agents

Traditional workflow automation follows a predefined path. Agents work toward a goal, selecting tools and steps as they go, then escalating to humans when confidence or authority runs out.

  • Goal-driven resolution of incidents, requests and cases
  • Orchestration across multiple agents and existing workflows
  • Platform-native context: CMDB, case history and knowledge in one place

Why the Platform Matters

An agent is only as safe as the system it acts inside. ServiceNow's advantage is that agents run on the same permissions, audit trail and process definitions that already govern enterprise work.

  • Role-based access and approvals apply to agent actions
  • Every action is logged against a ticket or case
  • Central visibility and governance of the AI estate is becoming a core capability

Where to Start

Pick a high-volume, well-documented process, measure baseline resolution time, and deploy an agent with human approval gates. Widen autonomy only as evidence accumulates.
