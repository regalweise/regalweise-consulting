AML for Digital Assets: Travel Rule, On-Chain Analytics and the Bank's Role

3 min read · April 2026 · RegalWeise Consulting

Digital assets create financial-crime risk, but also unusually transparent data. Banks that serve crypto businesses or hold tokens need controls designed for public ledgers rather than borrowed from card or wire monitoring.

The Regulatory Baseline

Global standards from the FATF extend AML obligations to virtual asset service providers, including the Travel Rule for sharing originator and beneficiary information. In India, VASPs were brought under PMLA obligations in 2023.

  • Customer due diligence on VASPs and their ownership
  • Travel Rule data exchange between institutions
  • Suspicious transaction reporting on digital asset activity

Using On-Chain Analytics

Blockchain analytics tools trace fund flows and score wallet risk. Combined with traditional transaction monitoring, they give a fuller picture.

  • Screen counterparties and wallet addresses against risk and sanctions exposure
  • Trace source of funds beyond the first hop
  • Document alert handling for regulators and audit

The Bottom Line

Treat digital asset AML as an extension of your KYC and monitoring architecture, with extra data sources, not a separate silo.
