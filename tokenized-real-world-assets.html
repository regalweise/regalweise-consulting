Tokenizing Real-World Assets: Funds, Bonds and Private Credit On-Chain

3 min read · July 2026 · RegalWeise Consulting

Tokenization represents ownership of traditional assets as digital tokens on a ledger. The appeal is operational: faster settlement, fractional access, automated servicing and 24/7 transferability.

The Asset Classes Leading the Way

Tokenized money market funds and government securities have attracted the most institutional interest, followed by private credit, real estate and commodities.

  • Money market funds used as on-chain collateral and cash management
  • Bonds issued natively on distributed ledgers
  • Private credit and fund interests with automated distributions

Design Questions to Settle Early

A token is a wrapper; its value depends on the legal and operational structure behind it.

  • Is the token the legal record of ownership or a mirror of it?
  • Who is the transfer agent, and how are corporate actions handled?
  • What secondary liquidity exists, and under which transfer restrictions?

The Bottom Line

Tokenization pays off when it removes real cost or unlocks real distribution. Test each candidate asset against that standard before building.
